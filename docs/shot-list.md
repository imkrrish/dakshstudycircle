# Shot list

Every photo the site needs, one row per slot. For the pages that already exist in data (Courses and the 9 course pages) the rows are the same words as the `media.shot` field in `src/content/pages/courses.ts` and `src/content/courses/*.ts`: if you change one, change the other. Until a photo is added, a "photo to come" card with this brief shows in development only; production leaves the photo out.

## Rules for every photo (phone is fine)

- Hold the phone **horizontal** (landscape), at **chest height**.
- Use the **1× lens**. No wide-angle (it bends the room and stretches faces at the edges).
- **Step back instead of zooming.** Zoom is a crop and loses quality.
- **Daylight, with the room lights on.** No flash, no night shots.
- Clear the board and the desks first. **No clutter, no balloons**, no stray bags or bottles.
- Take **3–4 frames of each shot**, slightly different positions. We pick the best.
- Shoot at the **highest resolution**. The file must be **at least 2400 px wide**. Send the original files (WhatsApp "document" or a cloud link), not a compressed copy.

## Consent

Get **written permission** (a message is enough) from every student whose face is visible, or from their parent or guardian for a minor. Shooting students **from behind** avoids this, so it is the default in the briefs below. Teachers and the director also give permission for portraits.
