# What to collect from the institute

Plain-word lists, one per content type, matching the fields in `src/lib/content/` (section and collection schemas).
Items marked `sample: true` in the data are examples; they show in development and are left out of
production until replaced with real data. `pnpm build` lists the ones still left.

## Faculty (one record per teacher)

For each teacher, please send:

1. **Full name** as they want it printed.
2. **Job title**, e.g. "Director", "Senior Physics teacher".
3. **Subjects** they teach, using the same names as the courses (Maths, Science, English, Hindi, Social Studies, Physics, …).
4. **Classes** they take (Class 9, Class 10, …).
5. **Qualifications**, one line (degrees, B.Ed., other certificates).
6. **Teaching experience**: the **year they started teaching** (the site works out "N years teaching" from it) and the **year they joined Daksh**. Only what is true and can be checked.
7. **A short bio**, two or three true sentences about how they teach. No ranks, no "best".
8. **A photo** and **permission to use it**: head and shoulders, daylight, plain background, taken at the centre. We never use
   AI-made or stock photos of people. Also say if they do not want a photo shown.
9. **Whether they are the director** (only one person).
10. **Order** they should appear in on the faculty page.

Still needed from the owner: Pathak Sir's full name, subject(s) and classes (we only know "Pathak Sir,
Science / Physics"); who teaches English, Hindi and Social Studies for Classes 9–10.

For Anil Kumar's bio: does he personally look after the lectures, the study material and the tests? (The site says nothing about this until you confirm.)

## Results (one record per student result)

Only real results the student or parent has agreed to publish. For each one, please send:

1. **Student's name** as it may be printed (and a yes that we may print it).
2. **Year** of the result.
3. **Class**: Class 10, Class 12, or an entrance exam.
4. **Exam**: CBSE, AISSEE, RMS CET, RIMC, …
5. **Stream** (Class 12 only): Science, Commerce or Humanities.
6. **Percentage**, for board results (as on the marksheet).
7. **Subject marks** (optional): subject and marks, from the marksheet.
8. **Outcome** (entrance results only): the school, and whether the student was Admitted or Selected.
9. **Which Daksh course** the student took (for example "Classes 9–10 tuition").
10. **A photo** (optional): with the student's or parent's written yes.
11. **Whether to feature it on the home page** (a handful of the best-known results).

## Results (for the /results page)

Could you please send the following? Students are minors, so nothing is shown without permission.

1. **Board toppers for recent years:** each student's name, class (10 or 12), stream for Class 12, year, overall percentage, and subject-wise marks if available.
2. **Entrance admissions:** each student's name, exam (RMS CET, RIMC or AISSEE), school and campus, entry class, and year.
3. **Written permission from parents** to show each student's name, marks and photo on the website. A message is enough.
4. **Photos:** one portrait per student, if the student and parents agree (see the portrait brief in `docs/shot-list.md`).
