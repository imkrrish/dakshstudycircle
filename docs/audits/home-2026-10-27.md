## Findings, prioritised

### P0: launch blockers (nothing is broken in dev)

1. **Mock results shown as real** · Results honour board
   - Problem: 18 invented student names, scores and admissions render with no mark. A visitor reads them as facts, which conflicts with PRODUCT.md principle 1 and the Indian rule on misleading coaching ads.
   - Fix: replace `src/content/results.ts` with real data before launch, or hide the section (and the "See all results" link). Do not ship while the data is mock.
2. **Canonical and og:url = `https://example.com`** · SEO / `site.ts`
   - Problem: `site.url` is a placeholder, so the canonical, `og:url` and metadataBase point at another site.
   - Fix: set the real domain in `site.ts`; add an `og:image` and make `twitter:card` `summary_large_image`.

### P1: fix before release

3. **AI-generated people on the page** · Director split, "Why Daksh" figure
   - Problem: both photos are AI images; DESIGN.md forbids AI people, and the classroom image has a visible error (triangle angles). Weakens trust where trust matters most.
   - Fix: swap for real photos (crop per DESIGN.md Portrait rules); until then keep them clearly internal.
4. **Unconfirmed claims stated as fact** · Why Daksh, FAQ, CTA band
   - Problem: PRODUCT.md lists "batch size / small groups", "syllabus finished early", "online classes", "free demo until you're satisfied" as *to confirm*, but the page states "Batches are small, about 10 to 20", "We finish the syllabus early in every class", "Your child can try our offline classes free". Also the 9–12 vs 8–12 conflict for Anil Sir.
   - Fix: get owner confirmation, then update PRODUCT.md and the copy together (`/impeccable clarify`).
5. **Mobile LCP 3.6 s (lab)** · Hero / global CSS
   - Problem: 22.6 KB render-blocking CSS plus font swap delay the first heading paint on throttled mobile (score 0.6 on LCP).
   - Fix: trim unused CSS and inline the critical part if the framework allows (check Next's `inlineCss` option); confirm fonts are used with `size-adjust` fallbacks so swap doesn't re-lay out the h1. Re-run Lighthouse on a real device or WebPageTest.

### P2: next pass

6. **Flame overlay image is 73 KB for a 23 px element** · Hero emblem (`emblem-flame.tsx`, `daksh-emblem-flame.png`)
   - Fix: export the flame at about 64x87 (or WebP/AVIF) and keep it lazy-after-idle; saves about 70 KB, the largest single image cost on the page.
7. **No-JS dead controls** · Navbar mobile menu, every "Talk to us" button, year tabs
   - Problem: with JavaScript off, the menu toggle shows but opens nothing, and "Talk to us" does nothing. Parents on cheap phones with blocked JS get no route to a human.
   - Fix: give contact-dialog buttons an `href="tel:…"`/`#contact` fallback (progressive enhancement), and make the mobile nav a native `<details>` or `:target` menu.
8. **Honour-board entrance row is cramped at 375** · Results board
   - Problem: "Admitted · Sainik School" in small caps takes most of the row, so "Aryan Thakur" wraps to two lines and the leader shrinks to a dot.
   - Fix: at `<sm`, let the admission wrap under the name, or shorten to "Sainik School" with the status in the meta line (`/impeccable layout`).
9. **Unused JS and legacy transpile** · Bundles
   - Fix: lazy-load the carousel and anime.js islands below the fold; set browserslist so 13 KB of polyfills aren't shipped (`/impeccable optimize`).
10. **Footer links under 24 px tall at desktop** · Footer (Lighthouse target-size fail on Privacy policy, 92x20; other footer links 22 px)
    - Fix: add vertical padding or `hit-area` so every footer link has at least a 24 px target (DESIGN.md's 2.25 rem desktop minimum is for primary actions, but WCAG 2.2 AA 2.5.8 needs 24 px).
11. **Focus on the results tab panel is invisible** · Results board
    - Problem: the `tabpanel` is focusable and takes a tab stop, but shows no ring (outline none).
    - Fix: give the panel `focus-ring`, or remove the tab stop (`tabIndex={-1}`) since its rows are focusable.

### P3: polish

12. **One lone-word heading** at 375: "Aryan Thakur" (honour board name). Join with ` ` in the data render, or `whitespace-nowrap` at small widths.
13. **Course stream chips** ("Science", "Commerce", "Humanities") wrap onto two rows in the Classes 11–12 card at 1280; consistent with DESIGN.md (max two rows) but looks ragged. Optional.
14. **DOM size 1,047 nodes.** The 18 result rows render all three years. Fine for crawlers; consider trimming expanded-row markup if mobile INP ever suffers.
15. **Dialog tab wrap:** after "Close", two Tab presses went to a non-dialog target before returning (probably Base UI focus guards in headless). Re-check in a real browser.

## Systemic patterns
- Content truth is the weakest layer: design tokens and structure are coherent; the risks are placeholder data and unconfirmed claims. Keep the launch gate in one checklist.
- Progressive enhancement is strong for content (everything renders with JS off) but not for actions.
- Several useful behaviours rely on custom focus handling (`has-[:focus-visible]` on cards); keep testing them when the shadcn components are updated.

## Positive findings
- Zero detector findings, zero horizontal overflow from 320 up, CLS 0, TBT 0, 506 KiB total transfer.
- One red button per screen holds at every scroll position checked; the dark CTA uses the paper button as specified.
- Type scale, heading levels and the 0.9375 rem floor are respected (no text under 15 px found).
- Full keyboard path works with consistent focus ring; dialog/drawer/menu restore focus; carousel and tabs follow the ARIA patterns.
- Honest empty-state handling: no fabricated numbers in the hero or proof line; FAQ answers pull hours, phones and address from site data.
- Reduced motion leaves nothing running.
